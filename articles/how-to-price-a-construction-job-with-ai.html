How to Price a Construction Job with AI (Step by Step)

Faster estimates, same accuracy — for any trade.

AI won't price a job for you — but it will cut the time you spend on everything around the pricing: the material list, the scope language, the proposal. Here's the workflow that keeps the speed without letting AI anywhere near the math it gets wrong.

The golden rule: AI is confident and often wrong at arithmetic. Never let it calculate your quantities or your final price. Use calculators for numbers; use AI for words.

Step 1 — Do the takeoff (numbers stay off the AI)

Measure the plans and get your raw quantities the way you always have. For the math — square footage, board feet, yards of concrete, waste factors — use a real calculator. The free BuildTally calculators cover concrete, drywall, flooring, insulation, paint, and roofing and run the actual formulas, so your quantities are solid before AI ever sees them.

Step 2 — Turn raw numbers into a clean material list

This is where AI earns its keep. Paste your measured quantities and let it organize:

"I'm doing a [trade] takeoff. Here are my raw numbers: [paste]. Build a clean material list with a [10]% waste factor added, organized by material type, waste shown as a separate line. Don't invent any materials or quantities."

Check that it used your numbers and didn't add anything. A waste factor of 5–15% is typical depending on material and layout.

Step 3 — Price it (your judgment, not the AI's)

Apply your unit costs, labor hours, markup, and tax. This is your expertise — the part a competitor with a chatbot can't copy. Do the arithmetic in a calculator or spreadsheet and eyeball every line.

Step 4 — Write the proposal in five minutes

Once you've got the scope and the number, hand them to AI:

"Write a clean, professional proposal. Company: [name]. Customer: [name]. Scope: [describe]. Price: [$X], [include/exclude] tax. Include a scope-of-work list, exclusions, payment terms of [50% deposit, balance on completion], and that the quote is valid for [30] days. Plain tone."

Read it, fix anything off, send. What used to take an hour takes a few minutes.

Frequently asked questions

Will AI replace estimators?

No. AI is fast at drafting and terrible at judgment and math. Pricing a job right still takes an estimator who knows the trade, the market, and the risks. AI just removes the busywork.

Which AI should I use?

ChatGPT or Claude — both have free versions that are plenty for this. Set up a pre-loaded assistant once so you don't re-explain your business every time.

How much time does this actually save?

Most of the savings are in proposals, material lists, and follow-up messages — the writing. For a busy estimator that's often several hours a week.
